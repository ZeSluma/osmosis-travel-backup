# ADR 0005 — Accepted gate order and safety policy reconciliation

2026-09-17 follow-up: [ADR0006](0006-cleanup-gate-and-authorization.md) accepts the GATE-9 placement and confirmation defaults proposed below and supersedes their pending status. Earlier text is retained as decision history.

2026-09-17. Accepted product/governance decisions from the user's three review documents. Architecture rebase accepted in principle, not an implementation authorization or hardware proof. Supersedes pending-decision statements in the initial rebase; historical evidence stays unchanged.

1. Adopt GATE-0 -> GATE-1 -> GATE-2 -> GATE-3 -> GATE-7 -> GATE-4 -> GATE-5 / GATE-6 -> GATE-8, retaining IDs. GATE-7 precedes GATE-4 because one-tap autonomous backup cannot be claimed until lifecycle-safe execution, automatic reconnect and recoverable synchronization are demonstrated. Accepted scopes and graph are in REVISED_GATE_DEPENDENCIES; completed records are not renumbered.
2. R-037 capture-day organization is mandatory, including immutable resolved paths, sidecar grouping and documented time-source/zone uncertainty. No new hardware proof is implied.
3. Default safety policy: verified phone landing copy plus at least one independently verified SSD/cloud copy, on distinct storage domains outside the camera. Two folders/partitions on one physical device are not independent. Configured extra mandatory destinations must also pass. Phone-only is not safe-to-clear. Future configuration cannot silently weaken this default.
4. Adopt six asset classes and scoped completeness predicates in ASSET_INCLUSION_POLICY v2. Unrelated unknown artifacts do not automatically fail camera sync; potentially required unknowns require disposition or safe preservation. Required recording members cannot be omitted to obtain success.
5. Clarify INV-001: never automatically delete camera originals. Future deletion requires explicit user initiation and confirmation of a precisely revalidated, redundantly verified source snapshot. INV-008 still means the SAFE TO CLEAR CAMERA status has no destructive side effect; a separate confirmed action may consume eligibility. No format operation belongs to cleanup. This is policy authorization to design, not authorization to delete now.
6. GPS Sync is optional recording telemetry, not a backup prerequisite. Structured sanitized diagnostics are always available in the target architecture; verbose Save logs is separate, opt-in, temporary and unnecessary for reliable backup.

Deletion is proposed as a new isolated **GATE-9 — Explicit verified-snapshot cleanup**, after GATE-4 and the GATE-5 and/or GATE-6 capability needed by the configured redundancy policy, with all earlier dependencies passed. GATE-8 zero-touch is independent; it can never trigger cleanup. GATE-9 placement remains a proposal for later approval. No real destructive use until dedicated fake/fault/security and S25/Pocket HIL criteria pass and implementation/use is explicitly authorized.

The GPS clarification mentions a possible deliberately enabled location diagnostic session, but also explicitly prohibits persisted coordinates and retains INV-005. This reconciliation selects the strict no-coordinate logging/export design. It does not introduce a location-logging exception. Any future exception requires an explicit separately reviewed policy change, not a verbose-mode toggle.

Gate-entry documentation is reconciled; GATE-1 itself remains NOT_TESTED. Permissions, dependency/security disposition and target capability assumptions still require its review. Hardware is PAUSED_BY_USER; implementation_authorized and release_allowed remain false.
