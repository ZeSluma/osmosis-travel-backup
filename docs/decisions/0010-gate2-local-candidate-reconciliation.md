# ADR0010 — Existing phone copies are persistent candidates

2026-09-17. User explicitly identifies discovery/association of pre-ledger Osmosis downloads as a G2 requirement. Earlier deferral of all local reconciliation to G3 is superseded; G3 retains stronger equivalence/integrity and verified promotion. Evidence: [G2 reconciliation](../evidence/GATE-2/2026-09-17_local-reconciliation/REPORT.md).

Use Room schema3 candidate rows and replica localPresence. Read accessible primary-phone MediaStore metadata only within existing Osmosis landing zones. Stat read-only descriptors for current length/change evidence; read no payload. Match folder plus original name/reserved path, exact positive known size and publication state as candidate evidence only. Persist the reference, rule, metadata-only confidence, observed metadata/change token and epoch. Missing, inaccessible, changed, pending, duplicate and cross-asset claims remain explicit; never choose the first ambiguous match, silently forget prior relationships or mint LOCAL_VERIFIED.

Local discovery is inside the serialized coordinator's fenced reconciliation flow. Complete accessible absence may schedule DOWNLOAD; plausible complete candidate schedules VERIFY_EXISTING; conflicting/unknown availability schedules REVALIDATE_IDENTITY. These are decisions, not transfer execution. G3 must resolve available verification evidence and decide reuse versus safe retransmission; no permanent skip or equivalence claim arises from metadata. Existing physical URIs remain separate from immutable reserved capture-day paths, so legacy files are not moved or re-downloaded merely to adopt the ledger.

Additive1→2→3 and2→3 migrations preserve existing data, candidate relationships survive process restart, and private/no-backup storage/privacy invariants remain. No dependency/Gradle change, transport/keepalive rewrite, permission broadening or camera deletion. Hardware post-upgrade recognition is NOT_TESTED until actual S25/Pocket observations; stable snapshots from earlier audits alone do not prove another enumeration generation or G2 PASS.
